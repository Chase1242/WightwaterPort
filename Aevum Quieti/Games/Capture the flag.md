# Capture the flag
- A river environment with rocky and vine-y portions
## Game rules
- Teams of 5 max, 3 min
- Player with flag can only make evasive moves
- Can't throw flag
	- Handoffs of flag allowed, as long as the pole of the flag is what is being passed
- First team to get flag to their side wins
- Can't harm other teams
- Marking 2 stress on a person forces them to spend the next round retreating to the original spot of their flag
- Must stay in bounds
- Countdown die starts at the following number:
	- 10-12 for easy
	- 8-10 for medium
	- 6-8 for hard
### Objective
- Bring a marker, usually a small, colored flag (green and red, blue and purple, etc.) to the teams own flag
	- Team's flag must be in team's possession or in original spot
## Prize 
- Common item from loot table per party member (1d12)
