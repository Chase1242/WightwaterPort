## Introduction
> The moon slowly rises above the horizon, and with it, night falls over the [[Holy Empire]]. As the sun sets and the stifling heat of the day leisurely fades, a wind whips up over the [[Osdean Mountains]] and hurtles down the steep, jagged cliffs.
> The wind, formally known as Gale, it thinks, passes over a sprawling city, one that looks haphazardly planned and rapidly grown; houses outside the city proper look constructed from mud, wood, or loose stone. Gale also, much to its dismay, catches a fetid smell, one made from conditions unfit to live in. Eventually, as Gale picks up speed through the winding streets, flowing smoothly through alleys lit by lanterns, it plays tricks, snatching a hat for a second and ruffling perfectly done hair, playing for a short moment with those it passes by. Once Gale loses interest in the those silly games, it rises and loses the stench of human waste, gently avoiding a large mountain in the middle of the city, and shortly thereafter, it reaches the free open ocean.
> Gale enjoys their time flowing over the water, sometimes lazily playing with the waves of the ocean, sometimes whipping spray up and into the air, infusing itself with water. Gale finds itself with other of its kind, and as wind does, feeds into themselves, twisting and flying into the sky in a frenzy. Lightning and rain joins the horde of wind, the water rejoining the ocean in sheets. 
> By and by, the fury of the storm slowed, becoming gales, then breezes. Gale, the zephyr born in the mountains of the old continent, relatively ancient by some unknown standards, reaches the New Continent. The sun and Gale arrive at roughly the same time, the heat banished by the moon, returning with a vengeance. 
> It flows over a new town, at the mouth of a river. This town, compared to the last, seems carefully planned, but still, the scent that seems to stick to humans is picked up by Gale.
