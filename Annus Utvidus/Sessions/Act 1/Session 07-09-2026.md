# [[Woodkin Druid's Grove]]
- At the tail end of [[Chapter 4 - Dragon Season]]
## Conflict between druid groves
- See [[Disease of the Forest]]
- The bloodshed can be paused if the party investigates the forest to discover the cause
### [[Crann Grove]]
- Believes the Woodkin are the party at fault, the soul of the forest is dying because of the commercial harvesting of trees
- Their own investigations have brought up no pattern to the disease
	- However, the rot only spreads in twilight (both morning and night)
- The nymphs have largely abandoned their grove, insisting the source is an unnatural, foreign magics
- [[The Handsome Fae (AKA Moonbean)|Moonbean]] has been active, planting ideas that the rot actually started when the Guild started, it's just accelerated recently
### Woodkin Grove
- Have found traces of unnatural magics as well
	- A deceased member (from before the conflict) was found to have traces of purple and blue magics
- Believe the Crann Grove have been practicing dark magics, due to a [[White Mask]] disguising self and bringing in verifiably false reports of dark magics
### Both
- Both believe the actual source to be in the remnants of the [[Faewild]], deep within Verdant Tangle
- Deep in the earth, beneath the [[Ancient Trees|Ancient Tree]], Strong Trunk, lay a magical mushroom circle, a portal to the Faewild, where ever it currently is
- Gilm offers to use this portal
